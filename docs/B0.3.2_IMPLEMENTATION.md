# B0.3.2 — Beauty & Performance

B0.3.2 develops the visual identity of Newb X Bare Visuals while carrying forward the performance work from B0.2.9. The goal is a cozy, polished shader that remains practical on Android, including the vivo Y22s. Performance gains are targets until measured in Minecraft.

## Profile lineup

The player-facing order is **Default**, **Pootaytoe**, **Bare Visuals**. The internal Prizma-inspired percentages are creative notes only and must never appear in pack descriptions or metadata.

### Default
The balanced main profile: natural daylight, warmer sunsets and evenings, believable soft-blue vanilla water, rounded clouds, restrained color, rain atmosphere, wet ground, night aurora, and the existing local emissive glow. It retains four rounded-cloud samples and uses a 32-unit terrain-reflection cap.

### Pootaytoe
The performance-focused profile, with more aggressive but targeted simplifications. It retains the recognizable sky, rounded clouds, rain, wet ground, and nighttime atmosphere while reducing optional per-pixel work. It uses two rounded-cloud samples, a 20-unit terrain-reflection cap, and skips optional glow leak/shimmer, weather speck styling, lava noise, underwater streaks, and the optional material-highlight approximation. This profile carries the practical low-cost principles originally discussed as Bare Mode; it is not an additional fourth subpack.

### Bare Visuals
The most expressive cozy profile, inspired by Prizma Visuals without copying it. It has softer blue daytime sky colors, peach/golden sunrise and sunset colors, readable transitions into night, rounded clouds, gentle atmospheric depth, and subtle material highlights. It keeps four rounded-cloud samples and uses a 30-unit terrain-reflection cap. It must not become oversaturated, orange all night, or expensive just for screenshots.

## Agreed visual and feature list

- **Performance Profiles:** implemented through the three subpacks, with real compile-time differences rather than names alone.
- **Bare Mode:** represented by Pootaytoe's leaner effect set and simplified rendering path. It is not a separate fourth profile.
- **Comfort-first visuals:** restrained saturation, readable block colors, natural sunlight, softer warm evenings, no forced brightness or distracting effects.
- **Night Visibility:** slightly lift the night palette and minimum-light contribution without turning night into daytime or adding expensive lighting passes.
- **Ore Visibility:** keep ores non-emissive; do not add glow or fake metallic reflections. The current shader path does not provide a reliable ore-material identifier, so a genuinely texture-only ore enhancement needs a verified texture/material route before it can be implemented safely. The small general low-light readability adjustment helps dark terrain, but must not be misrepresented as ore-specific detection.
- **Animation Controls:** preserve compile-time control points for animation features, but keep plant/lantern waving, tree waving, and water waves disabled as already agreed. No player-facing runtime animation toggle is claimed without a supported settings interface.
- **Custom Main Settings Entry:** scrapped and excluded. A normal resource-pack .mcpack does not by itself add a custom page to Minecraft's main Settings menu. Do not invent a UI or imply this was implemented.
- **PBR-inspired highlights:** a very cheap color-based approximation on bright, low-chroma opaque terrain for Default and Bare Visuals. It adds no texture reads and is not full PBR or an environment-reflection pass.
- **Water:** retain the vanilla-water rendering path; no custom water reflections, waves, or displacement.
- **Weather and atmosphere:** preserve lightweight rain particles, rain mist/fog, wet-ground darkening and puddle variation, rounded clouds, the existing sky pipeline, and readable nighttime aurora/clouds.
- **Existing exclusions:** no oversaturation, godrays, always-on water reflections, box clouds, plant/lantern waving, tree waving, or custom player/mob shadows. Do not remove established effects without evidence that a change is necessary.

## Performance foundation from B0.2.9

Carry forward the B0.2.9 terrain-reflection guard and range bounds, four-cardinal glow sampling, daytime aurora early-out, reduced cloud sampling in the performance profile, and profile-specific material compilation. Reuse these optimizations where they remain valid; do not assume they solve every frame drop. Focus comparisons on mountains, dense forests, distant terrain, dense builds, water, rain, thunderstorms, particles, and nighttime scenes.
